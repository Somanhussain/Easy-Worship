## 🔧 First-Time Setup Tips

Once Easy-Worship is running for the first time, here are some practical suggestions to get the most out of it:

**Take a minute to explore the options.**
The configuration utility is intentionally simple, so you can review every setting quickly. Read each label and adjust only what you understand — everything else can stay at its default.

**Set things up on the computer you will actually use.**
Configuration is stored on the machine where you run the utility, so set it up on the presentation machine rather than on an office laptop, unless the two are the same.

**Make changes before the service, not during it.**
Aim to finalize your configuration at least a day ahead. This gives you time to test your presentation slides and lyric displays without the pressure of a live audience.

**Keep a note of your settings.**
If your church uses more than one presentation computer, write down the settings you chose so you can replicate them quickly on the other machines.

**Ask one person to be the "settings owner".**
On a team with rotating volunteers, having one person responsible for configuration prevents accidental changes and keeps everything consistent.
